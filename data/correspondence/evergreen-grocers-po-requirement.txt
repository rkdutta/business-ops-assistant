Entity: Evergreen Grocers (customer)
Date: 2026-04-03
Title: Notes - invoicing requirements

Evergreen Grocers requires a valid purchase order (PO) number on every
invoice or it will be rejected by their accounts payable system and payment
will be delayed. Their AP department only accepts invoices submitted through
their vendor portal, not by email. Each of their store locations issues its
own PO numbers, so confirm which location placed the order before invoicing.
