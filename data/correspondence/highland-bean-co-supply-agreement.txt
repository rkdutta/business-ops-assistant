Date: 2026-05-12
Title: Master Supply Agreement - Payment Terms

Per our signed master supply agreement with Highland Bean Co, payment terms
are net-45 from invoice date. Pricing on green coffee beans is locked for a
12-month period from the agreement start date; any price changes require 60
days written notice before taking effect. Minimum order quantity per
shipment is 500kg. Highland Bean Co has agreed to hold buffer stock of our
top two varieties (Ethiopia and Colombia) to cover unexpected demand spikes.
