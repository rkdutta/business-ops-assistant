Entity: Sunrise Logistics (supplier)
Date: 2026-05-30
Title: Contract note - delivery SLA terms

Sunrise Logistics' delivery SLA requires 48 hours advance notice for any
large catering order dispatch (defined as orders needing a full pallet or
more). Standard deliveries are next-business-day. Missed SLA windows on
their end qualify for a 5% credit on that delivery's freight charge, which
must be requested within 7 days of the missed delivery.
